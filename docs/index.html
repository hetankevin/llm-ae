LLM-AE

A local command-line assistant for journal associate editors. Track submissions, assess manuscripts, find reviewer candidates, draft correspondence, and manage review deadlines.

Sign in with your OpenAI or Anthropic account and connect email through your browser. Gmail is registered and its desktop client is bundled. Google verification and live mailbox testing are pending; an unverified-app warning and 100-user cap currently apply. Outlook publisher registration is still pending.

Editorial decisions remain the associate editor’s responsibility. Messages require approval before sending. Routine reminders can be delegated explicitly.

Install

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hetankevin/llm-ae/main/install.sh | sh

Gmail requests read and send access. It does not request permission to delete or modify your mail. OAuth tokens stay in your operating system’s keychain. Manuscripts and workflow records stay in your local application directory, except content you explicitly choose to process with an external model provider.

Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026. This policy covers the open-source LLM-AE application distributed at github.com/hetankevin/llm-ae. LLM-AE is a local application; its maintainers do not operate a service that receives your mailbox, manuscripts, or model prompts.

Information the application uses

When you connect email, LLM-AE receives authorization tokens and checks the connected mailbox address. When you request synchronization, it reads messages in the selected folder or Gmail label within the configured lookback period. It uses sender and recipient addresses, subjects, message identifiers, bodies, and manuscript attachments to identify and track editorial correspondence. It does not retain the bodies of messages classified as unrelated. If you explicitly scan a folder for contacts, it retains observed names, email addresses, subjects, and interaction records to help identify reviewer candidates.

Gmail access uses the gmail.readonly and gmail.send permissions. The app reads messages without marking them read, and sends approved correspondence. It does not request Gmail modify or delete permissions. Microsoft email uses delegated IMAP and SMTP access. Routine reminders send only after you enable their delegation.

Storage and security

Mail authorization tokens are stored in your operating system’s keychain. Your local configuration stores references to those tokens. Editorial records, selected manuscript files, drafts, and audit records are stored in the local LLM-AE application directory. They are not uploaded to this website or to the maintainers. Protect your computer, backups, and local application directory as you would other confidential editorial material.

External services and model processing

LLM-AE communicates directly with your email provider to read mail and send approved messages. If you enable remote manuscript processing, content selected for an assessment or synthesis is sent to your configured OpenAI or Anthropic service through its API or official account CLI. That provider’s terms and account privacy settings apply. Before enabling it, ensure that your journal permits this processing and that the provider’s data-use settings meet your confidentiality obligations. Public reviewer searches send search terms to services such as arXiv or OpenAlex when you request them.

LLM-AE does not sell user data, serve advertisements, or use Google user data to train a general-purpose AI model. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your choices

You control the local data. To remove it, delete the application’s local data directory and the corresponding LLM-AE keychain entries. Revoke access in your Google or Microsoft account’s connected-app settings to stop future provider access. Deleting local tokens alone does not revoke an existing provider authorization. Removing local records does not remove copies already sent to recipients, saved in email, backed up, or processed by an external model provider; manage those copies with the relevant service.

This website and contact

This static website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web request information under its own privacy statement. The site adds no analytics or tracking scripts. For questions, contact the maintainer through the project’s issue tracker. Do not include private manuscripts, email content, or credentials in public issues.
